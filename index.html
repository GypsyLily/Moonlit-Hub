Current Direction

What is being worked on now

Moonlit Ledger

Ledger is now in a live invite-only multi-user beta. Current work is focused on hardening the beta: import and migration verification, stale-state protection, onboarding clarity, tester defects, and regression coverage.

Core scope remains trading-account operations, risk / guardrail tools, rules, journaling, live notes, sessions, reviews, and account overview. Brokerage telemetry remains a separate integration path where supported.

Moonlit Indicators

The indicator family is being reorganized around distinct components with explicit jobs: Currents, Trace, Ebb, and Levels, with VWAP Map retained in the broader product family.

Constellation is an umbrella/interface concept rather than another equivalent indicator, while Geometry remains planned. Broader experimental work remains separable from stable product-facing components.

Moonlit Replay

Replay remains intentionally independent from live account state. Its preserved baseline remains intact while further expansion is paused.

Moonlit Atlas

Atlas is currently in specification reconciliation. Its role is internal project memory and lineage: preserving products, decisions, branches, rationale, and “What led here?” relationships without rewriting history to look cleaner than it was.

Implementation begins only after the reconciled vocabulary and architecture are accepted.

Moonlit Hub

The public site is live and now serves as the curated presentation and future download surface for approved Moonlit releases. Ongoing maintenance is limited to public-safe product state, changelog, roadmap, screenshots, and approved release links.
