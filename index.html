Current Direction

What is being worked on now

Moonlit Ledger — beta hardening

Ledger remains an invite-only multi-user web beta. Current work focuses on reliable account and session state, safe imports, clearer onboarding, and verification of the beta release path before wider access.

Core scope remains account management, risk / guardrail tools, rules, journaling, live notes, sessions, reviews, and account overview. Brokerage telemetry and execution capture are separate future integration work, not features claimed for this beta.

Moonlit Indicators — source reconciliation

Indicator sources, historical research, and the development workflow have been organized. Current reconciliation focuses on Currents, Flow, Traces, and experimental Ebb, while Focus is evaluated as a static session-reminder panel and Levels remains a supporting component.

The next step is matching the exact TradingView implementations in use to their corresponding source records. A source used on a chart, a preserved file, a validated behavior, and a publicly approved build are different states.

Constellation remains an umbrella/interface concept, Geometry a future direction, and Echoes a parked concept. None is presented as a released component.

Traces — controlled behavior evaluation

Current research compares indicator output with manually identified chart relationships. The focus is reducing duplicate representations while preserving distinct levels, their origins, and consistent behavior over time.

A source audit and local-consolidation design inform controlled ES comparisons. This is evaluation work, not an announcement that consolidation is fixed, validated, or accepted as a stable build.

Prop Firm Stream Reference — data and feed validation

The working Windows prototype follows a simple lookup path: choose a firm, then Accounts or Firm Rules. Account lookup distinguishes programs, sizes, and variants; promotions remain separate from baseline rules and pricing.

Current work concerns source freshness and verification of the update/import workflow. Official sources take precedence for baseline-rule conflicts. The promotions workflow uses the agreed PropScore update stream with supporting official links; reference coverage is not an endorsement.

This is a standalone companion tool. Shared reference-data integration with Ledger remains planned, and the catalog does not claim automatic updates or complete, continuously verified coverage.

Moonlit Replay

Replay remains intentionally independent from live account state. Its preserved baseline remains intact while further expansion is paused.

Moonlit Atlas

Atlas remains in specification reconciliation as the internal project-memory and lineage system. Implementation begins only after the reconciled vocabulary and architecture are accepted.

Working development maps and evaluation boards remain internal tools, not public products or downloads.

Moonlit Hub

The public site is live. Maintenance keeps the catalog, current direction, roadmap, and approved distribution information aligned without publishing private source or internal project records.
