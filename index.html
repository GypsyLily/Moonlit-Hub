Current Direction

What is being worked on now

Moonlit Ledger

Continue consolidating trading-account operations around account management, risk/guardrail tools, rules, journaling, live notes, sessions, reviews, and account overview.

Multi-account telemetry may use NinjaTrader / Tradovate integrations where supported. Availability depends on the active implementation and platform capabilities.

Moonlit Indicators

Reorganize the product family around VWAP Map 1s, VWAP Map 1m, Automated Levels, Manual Levels, and product-facing research. Broader experimental work may later live in a separate Indicator Lab.

Moonlit Replay

Replay remains intentionally independent from live account state and is currently backburnered while active work concentrates elsewhere.
